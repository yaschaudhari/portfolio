# Kogmed Healthcare — Portfolio Screenshots

A full-stack clinic / hospital management platform (PHP + MySQL) with three role-based
surfaces: an **Admin / Staff console**, a **Doctor workspace**, and a **mobile-first
Patient portal**. Captured at full resolution (desktop 1440×900, mobile 390×844).
